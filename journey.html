Six weeks, step by step

Each week had one clear aim, from choosing the site to checking on the saplings.

  1. Week 1 · 1–6 June

    Getting to know the village

    Visited Gudur, observed trees and open spaces, marked possible plantation spots (roadsides, school surroundings, gardens) and drafted the survey questionnaire.

  2. Week 2 · 8–13 June

    Household survey

    Went door to door to ask about trees near homes, awareness of their benefits, interest in planting, and problems like tree cutting and poor upkeep.

  3. Week 3 · 15–20 June

    Awareness conversations

    Explained how trees clean the air and give oxygen, why existing trees should not be cut, and why saplings need regular watering.

  4. Week 4 · 22–27 June

    Planning the plantation

    Revisited the shortlisted places, spoke to residents about the difficulties of planting, and studied native species suited to local soil and climate.

  5. Week 5 · 29 June – 4 July

    Planting with residents

    Arranged saplings, prepared the sites and planted together with community members, then agreed who would look after each tree.

  6. Week 6 · 6–11 July

    Follow-up and feedback

    Checked the saplings, collected feedback, and noted challenges such as water availability and protection from damage.
