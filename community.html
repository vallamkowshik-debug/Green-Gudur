About Gudur

Gudur is a residential village where farmers, small business owners, private and government employees, daily-wage workers and students live side by side. It has homes, roads, schools, shops and the basic facilities people need every day.

Residents keep their customs, festivals and family ties, and share values that suit a plantation drive well.

  • Respect for elders and family
  • Helping neighbours
  • Joining local festivals and gatherings
  • Keeping public places clean
  • Respecting nature
  • Unity and cooperation
