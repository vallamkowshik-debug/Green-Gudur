Promoting Environmental Sustainability Through Tree Plantation and Community Participation

A six-week Community Service Project in Gudur village, Andhra Pradesh: surveying households, talking about trees, and planting saplings with local residents.

  • Gudur, Andhra PradeshProject community
  • 1 June – 26 July 2026Project period
  • Sree Venkateswara College of EngineeringNellore (Autonomous)

Potting a sapling at a household in Gudur.

Project overview

Trees clean the air, hold soil in place, give shade and shelter wildlife. Growing construction, tree cutting and low awareness have reduced green space in many villages, Gudur included.

Our project set out to change that locally. We observed the village, found places where trees could grow, surveyed residents, ran awareness conversations, and planted saplings together with the community, then went back to see how they were doing.

The main message: protecting the environment is not only a job for government. It is shared by every household, and planting is only the start. Saplings need watering, protection and someone who takes responsibility for them.

  • Environmental awareness
  • Teamwork
  • Leadership
  • Communication
  • Survey skills
  • Social responsibility

What we set out to do

  • Raise awarenessExplain how trees reduce air pollution, give oxygen, prevent soil erosion and support biodiversity.
  • Listen to residentsUse a 12-question survey and household visits to learn what people know and what stops them planting.
  • Plant with the communityChoose suitable places and saplings, and plant alongside local people rather than for them.
  • Make care a habitShow why watering, fencing and regular care decide whether a sapling survives.
