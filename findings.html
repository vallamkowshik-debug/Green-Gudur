What we found

Problems in the community

  • Too few trees and green spaces in some parts of the village
  • Trees cut for construction and other activities
  • Low awareness about tree plantation
  • Rising dust and vehicle pollution, and higher heat as green cover shrinks
  • Saplings left without protection or regular watering
  • Limited resident, school and youth participation
  • Plastic waste dumped in open areas

What the project achieved

  • Residents understand more clearly why trees matter
  • People are more willing to plant and protect saplings
  • Students and young people showed interest in environmental work
  • Places needing extra greenery were identified
  • Residents recognised their own role in keeping trees alive

What should happen next

Recommendations for residents, the school and the Gram Panchayat.

Short term

  • Hold awareness programmes on trees and the environment
  • Plant in schools, along roadsides and in public places
  • Give saplings to interested families
  • Water and protect new saplings regularly
  • Put up posters and banners

Long term

  • Every household plants and looks after at least one tree
  • Yearly plantation programmes
  • Local groups that monitor tree growth and survival
  • Ask the Gram Panchayat for saplings and watering support
  • Stop unnecessary tree cutting
  • Regular environmental sessions in schools

Conclusion

Tree plantation is not only about planting trees but also about protecting and maintaining them. When students, youth and residents work together, a village can grow greener, healthier and more sustainable for the next generation.
